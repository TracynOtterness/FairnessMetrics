About

This tool visualizes the Impossibility Theorem of Fairness (Chouldechova/Kleinberg): when base rates differ between populations, you cannot simultaneously achieve equal PPV and equal FPR/FNR.

💡 Try keeping distributions identical but changing prevalence to see the theorem in action.
